Frequently Asked Questions

What can I watch on TrueVisions NOW?
TrueVisions NOW gives you access to live TV, movies, Thai & international series, kids content, sports, and TrueVisions Originals — all in one app. The content available depends on your plan.
What devices does TrueVisions NOW support?
You can stream on Smart TVs, Android & iOS phones and tablets, web browsers, Chromecast, Apple TV, and more. Visit our Devices & Apps support page for the full list.
Can I download content to watch offline?
Yes — downloads are available on Ent 299 and MAX plans. You can save titles to your device and watch them without an internet connection. Downloaded content expires after 30 days or when your subscription lapses.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Absolutely. There's no lock-in contract. Cancel any time from your account settings and you'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period.
